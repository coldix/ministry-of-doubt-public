# Working Method

Operational protocol for research notes and chapter drafts. Slogans are not enough; this is the scorecard.

**Canonical full method (experimental):** [truth-protocol.md](truth-protocol.md) — *Trivium+ Protocol* (Grammar → Logic → Rhetoric → Reality test → Independence → Adversarial review → Update). Use that document for stage-by-stage procedure; this file remains the short scorecard and category table.

**Worked examples:** [worked-examples.md](worked-examples.md)

## Categories of explanation

Use the narrowest category that fits the evidence. Do not jump categories without new evidence.

| Category | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **Documented conspiracy** | Secret coordination + intent + concealment, later evidenced |
| **Cover-up** | After-the-fact concealment or minimisation; may or may not imply the preferred original crime |
| **Institutional failure** | Negligence, groupthink, careerism, bad process |
| **Capture** | Regulator/media/science bent by money, politics or status without needing a smoke-filled room |
| **Propaganda / narrative management** | Public influence; sometimes legal, sometimes covert |
| **Open uncertainty** | Incomplete data under active dispute |
| **Noise / error** | Mistakes, statistical flukes, incompetence |
| **Unfalsifiable myth** | Immune to disconfirmation; anomaly always “proves” the theory |

## Evidence hierarchy (default)

1. Primary documents with provenance (archives, court records, contemporaneous memos)  
2. Physical/forensic measurement with chain of custody and methods  
3. Sworn testimony under adversarial process  
4. Multiple independent contemporaneous sources  
5. Official reports (note mandate, limits, politics)  
6. Named investigative journalism with documents  
7. Anonymous sources, leaked secondary summaries  
8. Pattern inference, statistical anomaly claims  
9. Motive speculation, cinematic coherence  

Anomaly hunting without a generative model ranks low.

## Updating rules

- One demonstrated lie changes **source credibility** for related unsupported claims; it does not set all related claims to true or false.
- Updates are **local and proportional**. Tonkin does not raise the prior on moon-hoax.
- Missing evidence is not evidence for your preferred filler.
- “They control the evidence” increases the **transparency burden** on them; it does not substitute for positive evidence of your theory.
- Coordinated-looking outcomes: first model incentives, bureaucracy, fear, shared training and selection effects; require positive evidence of secret coordination before asserting conspiracy.
- Real crises can be exploited without having been created—score exploitation and creation separately.

## Commissions, leaks, FOIA

- A commission is an instrument with a mandate, deadline, political environment and limited powers—not a church and not automatically a whitewash.
- A bad process can still reach a correct main conclusion; a good process can still err.
- Leaks are evidence *about documents and programmes*; the leaker’s full worldview is not thereby validated.
- Destroyed or withheld records are process facts. They open questions; they do not auto-complete a favourite story.

## Hanlon’s razor and its abuse

Prefer incompetence when evidence of intent is weak. **Do not** use the razor to ignore memos, budgets, orders and repeated patterns that show intent (tobacco, COINTELPRO, MKUltra).

## Language discipline

Avoid: “everyone knows,” “wake up,” “sheeple,” “the science is settled” as a conversation-ender, “do your own research” as a substitute for method, and plot-like “they” without named agents and mechanisms.

Prefer: agents, incentives, documents, measurements, confidence intervals in prose, and explicit uncertainty.

## Negative controls

Periodically apply the full method to a claim that is popular in conspiracy culture and poorly evidenced (e.g. Apollo hoax, Illuminati master-control). If the method cannot kill weak claims, it is not a method.
